### Question 4: Grammar — Articles (-chen/-lein neuter rule)

### Question 5: Grammar — sein conjugation + ein/eine gender agreement

**Prompt:** How would you say "I am a woman" in German?

**Analysis:**

- 🟡 "ein Frau" → "eine Frau" (grammar — indefinite article must agree with gender; die Frau is feminine → eine) (category: grammar, subcategory: indefinite_article_gender)

### Question 6: Integration — Self-Introduction

**Prompt:** Write a short self-introduction (greeting + "Ich bin" + name + "ich bin ein/eine ___" description).
**Your answer:** "Ich bin Macus. Mein name ist Macus. Heute ist ein guten tag. Ich bin ein Fotograf, Pilot, und Biologe."
**Correct answer:** "Ich bin Macus. Mein Name ist Macus. Heute ist ein guter Tag. Ich bin Fotograf, Pilot und Biologe."

**Analysis:**
- ✅ "Ich bin Macus." / "Mein Name ist Macus." — correct structure.
- ✅ Consistent gender agreement with earlier exercise (masculine self-reference).
- 🟢 "name" / "tag" → "Name" / "Tag" (spelling — all German nouns are capitalized) (category: spelling, subcategory: capitalization)
- 🟡 "ein guten Tag" → "ein guter Tag" (grammar — attributive adjective needs -er ending for masculine nominative after "ein", not -en) (category: grammar, subcategory: adjective_declension)
- 🟡 "ein Fotograf, Pilot, und Biologe" → "Fotograf, Pilot und Biologe" (grammar — professions after "sein" drop the article entirely in German; also no comma before "und" in a list) (category: grammar, subcategory: predicate_noun_article)

**Score:** 7/10
